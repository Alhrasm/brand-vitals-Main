Privacy Policy

Your data is treated the way we treat our clients' budgets: carefully, transparently, and only for the purpose you gave it to us.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

Brand Vitals is a digital marketing company based in Cairo, Egypt, serving clients across Egypt and the MENA region. This policy explains what we collect through brandvitals.io and how we use it.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you submit through our forms (name, email, phone, company).
  • Business context you choose to share (goals, budget range, messages).
  • Usage analytics (pages visited, device, approximate location) via privacy-respecting analytics tools.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your consultation requests, the purpose you gave it to us.
  • To improve the website experience and our services.
  • We do not sell your personal data. Ever.

4. Legal bases & regional compliance

We process personal data in line with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 and applicable data-protection principles. Processing is based on your consent, our legitimate interest in responding to your inquiry, or contractual necessity.

5. Retention & sharing

We keep inquiry data only as long as needed to serve you, then delete it. We share data only with service providers essential to running this site (hosting, analytics, CRM) under strict agreements, never with advertisers.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Email info@brandvitals.io or message us on WhatsApp, and we respond within 30 days.

7. Terms of use

Content on this site, including text, brand assets and design, belongs to Brand Vitals and may not be reproduced without permission. Any results or figures we share reflect specific client engagements and are not a guarantee of future performance. This site is provided as-is; we may update it and this policy at any time, with the latest version always published here.
