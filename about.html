About Brand Vitals

Built in Cairo.
Working across MENA.

Brand Vitals is a full-service marketing agency based in Cairo, Egypt. We bring together strategy, creative, digital experience, and performance marketing to help ambitious businesses grow. Our approach is simple: understand the business first, build the right system, then measure what happens.

01 · Our story

Built for two languages. Fluent in both.

Most agencies chase impressions. We chose a harder discipline: we connect every campaign, every post and every pound of ad spend to a business number someone actually cares about, like revenue, orders and qualified leads.

Rooted in Egypt and fluent across MENA, we grew up bilingual by necessity and data-driven by choice. Today that means Arabic-native creative, English-grade rigor, and experience spanning e-commerce, real estate, healthcare, F&B, insurance and retail.

02 · How we think

Principles we don't negotiate.

01

Evidence before opinion

Every recommendation we make can be traced to data. When the data is silent, we test, we don't guess with your budget.

02

Your P&L is our KPI

Reach and engagement are means, not ends. We report on the numbers that appear in your books: revenue, cost per acquisition, return on spend.

03

Arabic-native, world-grade

Our Arabic is written, never translated. Our standards are global. A regional CEO and an international CMO should both feel at home with our work.

04

Transparency, even when it stings

If a channel underperforms, you hear it from us first, with the fix already in motion. Trust is built in the uncomfortable updates.
